Kuchu & Cutu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version: we don't collect anything. What you create in Kuchu & Cutu lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, shared only with the partner you invite.

What the app stores

Your names, start date, moods, notes, doodles, answers, countdowns, bucket list, date-idea swipes, game moves, the photos you add to Memories, and (only if you turn it on) an approximate location. It is stored on your device and in a private iCloud database that belongs to you.

Sharing with your partner

When you invite your partner, the app uses Apple's CloudKit sharing so the two of you can read and write the same shared space in iCloud. Only the person who accepts your invite can join; after they do, the invite link stops working for anyone else. You can unpair at any time.

What we (the developer) can see

Nothing. We run no servers, have no accounts, and include no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. CloudKit data in your iCloud is not visible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Location

Optional and off by default. If you turn on distance, the app asks for "while using" location with reduced accuracy, rounds it to about 1 km, and shares only that and your city name with your partner. It updates only when you tap Update. It never runs in the background.

Photos

You pick photos with the system photo picker; the app does not get access to your library. Picked photos are stored in your shared iCloud space.

Intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, extra date ideas may be generated by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.

Your controls

Settings → Export data gives you everything you've created. Settings → Delete all data removes it from your device and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes local data; you can also manage iCloud data in the Settings app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
