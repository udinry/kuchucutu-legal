Kuchu & Cutu

A free, private little app for two people in love: days together, doodles on each other's widgets, daily questions, memories and games.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email dev@udbhav.uk with your iOS version. Replies usually within two working days.

Common questions

How do we connect? One of you opens Settings → Invite your partner and sends the link. The other taps it on their phone and accepts. You both need to be signed in to iCloud.

Do we need an account? No. Kuchu & Cutu uses your own iCloud accounts to sync, so there's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log out of.

Is anything paid? No. Every feature is free. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Can we use it alone first? Yes. Everything works solo; when your partner joins, your notes, memories and countdowns are shared.

How do I export or delete our data? Settings → Export data, and Settings → Delete all data. To stop sharing, Settings → Unpair.

The widget isn't updating. Open the app once; iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule.
